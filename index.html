Three things to know first ​

  1. The app tells you what to do next. Every job has a blue next-step button at the top (for example Save quote → or Release car →). Press it and the app takes you to the right place.
  2. Follow the names, not the colours. This guide uses the exact words on the buttons and tabs, in bold. The look of the app may change; the names stay.
  3. If a button is grey, the app says why. Read the line next to it. It names the missing step, for example "Save quote is off: add a work line first."

One job, start to finish ​

The path almost every job takes:

Booked → Quoted → Under repair → Quality check → Ready for pickup → Released → Done

The desk guide walks through it in 8 short steps. Insurance and corporate jobs add a step or two. See Insurance jobs and Corporate & government jobs.
