How to use this guide ​

  • Find your role in the cards above, or read Chapter 1 for the role table and how the six hubs work.
  • Follow the workflow, not the screen layout. Steps name the hub, page, tab, status and the required field. Those stay the same when the look of the app changes.
  • Search with the box at the top (press / or Ctrl+K).
  • What changed? See What's new, including a one-job walkthrough from intake to release.
  • Live int environment for practice: Ops App https://rakkii-ops-int.web.app, Customer Portal https://rakkii-web-int.web.app.

Job status at a glance ​

The main path of every job:

Booked → Quoted → Under repair → Quality check → Ready for pickup → Released → Closed

Side statuses you will also see: New inquiry, For canvass, Waiting for LOA, Authorized, Waiting for parts, On hold, Invoiced, Cancelled, No-show. Chapter 5 has the full map.

All 13 chapters ​
