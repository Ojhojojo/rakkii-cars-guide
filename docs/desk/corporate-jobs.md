# Corporate & government jobs

**Who:** Owner or admin · **Adds to:** the [8 job steps](/desk/job/01-open-a-job)

Companies, government offices and bidding jobs usually pay **on terms** against a **PO** (purchase order), so the car can leave before the money arrives.

## What's different from a normal job

### 1. Set up the company once
In **Workshop › Customers**, add the company with its TIN, billing address and **Credit terms (days)**. For a PO that covers many jobs, create it in **Finance › Blanket POs**.

### 2. Open the job
On **New job**, set **Nature** to Corporate, Government or Bidding, and pick the company and car.

### 3. Record the PO
On the **Pay** tab, card **Nature and PO**: enter the **PO number**, **Approved PO amount** and **PO approved on**, then **Save**.

### 4. Upload the paperwork
Your shop may require documents before work starts (a PO, BAC resolution or notice of award). Upload them on the **Files** tab with the right **Kind**. The job tells you what's missing.

Owner only: to start without them, open **Start work without the usual documents** on the **Files** tab and save a reason.

### 5. Repair and bill as usual
Assign, start work, QC, then **Bill customer ₱…**. The invoice prints in the company's name. If a withholding percent applies, the **Summary** shows the expected net. Record the amount that actually arrives.

### 6. Release on terms
Once the customer invoice is **posted**, the car can be released without payment: "Can the car leave?" shows the balance as on credit terms. The open balance moves to **Finance › Who owes us** with its due date.

::: warning No invoice, no release on terms
Without a posted invoice the normal hold applies. Bill first.
:::
