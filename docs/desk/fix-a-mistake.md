# Fix a mistake

**Who:** Owner or admin

Most things in the app can be corrected, but money records are never edited or deleted. Instead you reverse them, so there's always a trail. Find your mistake below.

| Mistake | Fix | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong work line | Edit or delete the line, then **Save quote** again | Work › Work lines |
| Wrong technician | Pick the right one, **Save technicians** | Work › Team |
| Wrong date (car in, done, billed…) | **Edit dates** | Work › Dates |
| Wrong target date or appointment | **Change target date or appointment** | Overview |
| Wrong car or customer details | **More ▾ › Car** or **Customer** | Job header |
| Wrong status | Use **Back to shop**, **Back to quote** or **Resume** | The card's status buttons |
| Invoice amount too high | Issue a **credit note** (below) | Pay › Invoices |
| Invoice posted by mistake | **Void** it (below) | Pay › Invoices |
| Payment entered wrong | **Void** the payment and record it again | Pay › Payments |
| Money must go back to the customer | **Refund** (below) | Pay › Payments |
| Released too early | Owner presses **Unlock** | Job header |

## Credit note: lower what an invoice bills

1. On **Pay › Invoices**, press **Credit** on the posted invoice.
2. Enter the amount (including VAT), the date and a reason, then issue it.
3. **Print** the credit note for the customer or insurer.

No money moves. Balances, receivables and commission go down. **Void credit** reverses it.

## Refund: give money back

1. On **Pay › Payments**, press **Refund** on the payment.
2. Enter the amount, method, the shop account it came from, the date, a reference and a reason.
3. Save. **Void refund** undoes a refund entered by mistake.

## Void an invoice

Press **Void** on the invoice and give a reason. If it has credit notes or refunds, void those first.

## Unlock a released job

Owner only. Press **Unlock** in the job header. The old release is kept as **Previous release** history so you can see what changed. Release the car again when you're done.

::: warning Closed months
Once the owner closes a month (**Finance › Month close**), money in that month can't be changed. Corrections go in the current month.
:::
