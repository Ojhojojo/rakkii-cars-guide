# 3. Get the customer's OK

**Who:** Owner or admin · **Where:** Work tab › **Quote** card, and the **Pay** tab

The customer approves the estimate on their own phone. You may also take a deposit before work starts.

## Send the estimate

1. On the **Quote** card, press **Email quote**, or use **More ▾ › Copy customer link** and send the link by SMS or Messenger.
2. The customer opens the link on the Customer Portal. They see the job, the car and the total.
3. They press **Approve estimate** or **Decline estimate**. No money is taken.
4. You'll see it on the job's next-step button and in **Workshop › Customer activity**.

If the customer agrees in person or by phone, just carry on to the next step.

::: info Declined?
Change the work lines and save the quote again, or cancel the job from **More ▾ › Cancel job** (a reason is required).
:::

## Take the deposit (if your shop needs one)

Private jobs default to a **50%** deposit. Your shop decides whether the deposit blocks anything: nothing, the start of work, or release. The **Pay** tab says which in plain words.

1. Open the **Pay** tab.
2. Under **Record a payment**, set **Who paid**: Customer, the **Amount**, and the **Method** (Cash, GCash, Maya, Bank transfer, Card or Other).
3. For a bank transfer, choose the **Bank account** and fill in **Reference**.
4. Press **Record payment** and confirm.
5. Print an acknowledgement from **Work › Job order › More papers › Payment acknowledgement**.

The **Summary** card shows **Deposit still due** until it's covered.

::: tip Different deposit for this job?
Open **Job money settings › Down payment** on the Pay tab, press **Change the down payment**, enter a percent or amount with a reason, and press **Save down payment**.
:::
